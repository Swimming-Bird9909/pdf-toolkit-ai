Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before uploading your first PDF.

Is PDF Toolkit AI really free?

Yes. Every tool is completely free to use — no signup, no daily limits, no hidden fees. We may add optional paid services later, but the core tools will stay free.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No — and we mean it. Open your browser's Network tab and you'll see zero file uploads. Everything happens locally using pdf-lib, pdf.js, and Tesseract.js. Your files never leave your device.

Do I need to create an account?

No signup required. We hate signup walls as much as you do. Just open the tool and start working.

How large a PDF can I process?

There's no enforced file-size limit. Very large files (over ~200MB) may be slow on phones or low-RAM devices — use a desktop browser for best results.

What does "remove watermark" actually do?

We use two strategies: (1) strip true PDF watermark objects (the embedded overlay layer), and (2) AI-based masking of rendered watermark pixels. Strategy 1 works for true watermarks added in Acrobat/Word. Strategy 2 works for "burned-in" watermarks but is best-effort.

Can I use the AI Summary without an account?

Yes — for short documents, summary runs entirely on-device. For longer documents, we route through OpenAI / Anthropic APIs but your file is sent as base64 over HTTPS, not stored, and not used for training. Set your own API key in settings to use your own account.

Is there an offline / desktop version?

The PWA (install from your browser's address bar) works fully offline for compress, merge, split, metadata, remove-pages, and extract-images. OCR and AI Summary need internet for the AI models.
