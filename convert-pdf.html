Frequently asked questions

Common questions about convert pdf.

Which output format should I choose?

PDF to Word for documents you need to edit. PDF to Excel for tables and financial data. PDF to JPG for sharing on social media or embedding in slides. OCR for scanned/image-only PDFs.

Will formatting be preserved?

For digital PDFs (born-digital), text and basic paragraph structure are preserved. For scanned PDFs, use OCR first to get editable text. Complex layouts (multi-column, headers) are best-effort.

Are there file size limits?

There's no enforced file-size limit. Files over ~200MB may be slow on phones or low-RAM devices. Everything processes locally, so performance depends on your device, not our servers.
