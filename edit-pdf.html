Frequently asked questions

Common questions about edit pdf.

Will my PDF quality drop after editing?

No. Our editor uses lossless pdf-lib re-saves. Fonts, images, and vector graphics are preserved byte-for-byte.

Can I reorder pages by drag-and-drop?

Yes — in the Merge tool, drag thumbnails to reorder before combining. In Remove Pages, click to toggle pages for deletion.

Does the output have a watermark?

Never. We don't add watermarks to any output. That's the whole point of being free and private.
