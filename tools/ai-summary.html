About this tool

Get a concise AI summary of any PDF. Perfect for contracts, research papers, and reports. Free, private, runs on-device.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser using pdf-lib and pdf.js. Your file never touches a server. Open DevTools → Network and you'll see zero traffic from this page.

Tips

  • Use Chrome, Edge, or Safari for the best performance.
  • For very large files (100+ pages), close other tabs to free RAM.
  • Need batch processing? Upgrade to Pro — unlimited files, up to 500MB each.
