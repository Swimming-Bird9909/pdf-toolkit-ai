How to compress a PDF online

  1. Drop your PDF into the box above (or click to browse).
  2. Pick a preset — 100KB, 200KB, email-ready, or custom.
  3. Click Compress PDF. Files are processed in your browser, never uploaded.
  4. Download the result. Done.

What compression does

We re-render each PDF page at a reduced DPI and re-encode embedded images at a lower JPEG/WebP quality until the file fits your target. Text stays crisp because we never re-rasterize text layers.

Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. No upload, no server. Open DevTools → Network and you'll see zero traffic. Files are never seen by us or anyone else.
