About this tool

Build a clean, ATS-friendly resume in minutes. The PDF is generated with proper semantic structure (no fancy graphics that confuse applicant tracking systems), single-column layout, and standard section headings.

Why this is ATS-friendly

  • Single-column layout — most ATS engines read left-to-right, top-to-bottom. Multi-column layouts confuse them.
  • Standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills) — the exact keywords ATS engines look for.
  • No tables, no text boxes, no images — pure text the parser can extract.
  • Standard fonts (Helvetica) — available on every device, never substituted.

Features

  • Unlimited experience and education entries
  • Live preview as you fill in the form
  • Multiple professional templates (coming soon)
  • No watermark, no branding
  • Print-ready A4 PDF

Privacy

  • Everything happens in your browser. Your personal info, work history, and education never touch a server.
  • The PDF is built locally with pdf-lib. Your data stays yours.

Frequently asked questions

Will this resume pass ATS systems?

Yes. The generated PDF uses single-column layout, standard section headings, parse-safe fonts, and no fancy graphics — the exact requirements for 95%+ of applicant tracking systems.

Can I add a profile photo?

Photo upload is intentionally omitted — most ATS systems and US/EU recruiters recommend against photos to avoid bias. Add a photo only if specifically required for the role.

What template is used?

A clean, single-column template with bold name, italic title, contact info, and sections separated by horizontal rules. Optimized for both ATS and human readers.

Can I customize the layout?

Color and font customization is on the roadmap. For now, all resumes use the same professional template — designed to pass ATS and look clean to humans.
