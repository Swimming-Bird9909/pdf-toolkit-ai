Why merge PDFs?

Combining several PDFs into one keeps related documents together: a cover letter + resume, a scan + signed page, monthly reports into a yearly archive. One file is easier to email, upload, and store than six.

The fast way (free, no upload)

Open our Merge PDF tool, drop two or more files, drag to reorder, and click Process. It runs in your browser with pdf-lib — your files never touch a server, and the output has no watermark. Free, unlimited files, no daily cap.

Best for: Contracts, invoices, scans, reports — anything you want in one tidy file.

Method 2: Adobe Acrobat (paid)

Acrobat → Combine Files. Reliable, but $22.99/month and cloud-based. Watermark-free only on paid plans.

Method 3: Preview (Mac, free, local)

Open one PDF, drag others into the sidebar, export as PDF. Free and local, but clunky for many files and easy to mess up page order.

Common gotchas

"Pages came out in the wrong order"

Reorder the file thumbnails in the tool before you Process — drag them up or down. The final PDF follows the order you set.

"The file is too big to email"

Merge first, then run Compress PDF to shrink the combined file under your mail provider's limit.

"I see a watermark"

Some free mergers stamp their logo on every page. Ours does not — the output is clean.

Privacy

Our merger runs locally with pdf-lib. Files never leave your browser. For confidential bundles — client decks, signed contracts — that is the safe choice.
