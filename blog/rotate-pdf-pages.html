How to rotate PDF pages
— fix upside-down scans

Someone scanned a contract sideways. Page 3 is upside down. Here's how to spin individual pages (or the whole file) 90°, 180°, or 270° — without a paid app and without uploading anything.

Rotate one page vs. all pages

Most scanners let you rotate the view, but that doesn't change the file — reopen it and it's sideways again. To actually fix it, you rotate the page data itself. Our rotate tool lets you target a single page or apply one angle to every page at once.

The fast way (browser, no upload)

Open the tool, drop your PDF, and you'll see a thumbnail per page. Click a page, pick 90° / 180° / 270°, and it previews instantly. Hit "Rotate all" if the whole scan is off by 90°. Download — done. The file never leaves your tab.

Best for: Sideways scans, upside-down pages from a finicky feeder, mixed-orientation bundles.

Method 2: Preview (Mac, free)

macOS Preview → open PDF → View → Thumbnails. Select pages, then Tools → Rotate Left/Right. Works great and is already on your Mac. Downside: Windows and mobile users don't have it.

Method 3: Adobe Acrobat (paid)

Acrobat → Organize Pages → Rotate. Reliable, but it's $22.99/month and pushes your file to Adobe's cloud.

Common gotchas

"I rotated it but it's still sideways"

You probably rotated the view only. Make sure you export/save after rotating the page data, not just the on-screen preview.

"Only some pages are wrong"

Select just those thumbnails and rotate them individually — don't hit "rotate all", or you'll flip the good pages too.

"The text looks blurry after rotating"

Rotating shouldn't lose quality (it's a transform, not a re-render). If it looks soft, the source scan was low-res; try re-scanning at 300 DPI.

Privacy

Rotation runs locally with pdf-lib. Your document stays in your browser — safe for contracts, tax forms, and anything confidential.
